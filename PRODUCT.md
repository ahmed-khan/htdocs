# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three overlapping audiences, all visiting connectingahmad.info:

- **Academic peers and collaborators** — researchers, journal editors, conference organizers evaluating research output and potential partnerships. They scan publications and certifications first.
- **Students and prospective candidates** — people considering courses, lab membership, or PhD supervision. They need to understand both expertise and approachability.
- **Industry and institutions** — companies, grant bodies, and institutions evaluating credentials for consulting, advisory roles, or senior academic appointments. They want authoritative signals fast.

No single audience dominates. The site must land clearly for all three without hedging.

## Product Purpose

Dr. Ahmad Raza Khan's academic portfolio: biography, research interests (cloud computing, machine learning, AI, cybersecurity), publications, certifications, services, and contact. Deployed at connectingahmad.info and hosted on AWS Lightsail via GitHub Actions CI/CD.

Success: any visitor — academic, student, or industry professional — immediately understands his field leadership, trusts his credentials, and knows how to engage him.

## Brand Personality

Credible, contemporary, and quietly confident. Dr. Khan is an internationally recognized academic who leads in cybersecurity and AI without needing to announce it. The site reads like a well-published researcher who also cares about communication: rigorous in structure, clear in voice, warm enough to invite collaboration.

Three physical-object words: **the cover of a respected international journal that's actually well-designed**.

## Anti-references

- **Generic Bootstrap professor sites** — off-the-shelf blue-and-white templates that look identical to every other academic's page. The current site is close to this; it's the primary thing to move away from.
- **SaaS and startup pages** — dark mode, gradient text, hero metric grids, glassmorphism. Wrong register entirely for an academic portfolio.
- **Dense institutional sites** — wall of links, no visual hierarchy, bureaucratic. Functional but signals zero craft and no personality.
- **Over-designed personal brand sites** — heavy scroll animations, oversized display type, agency-style theatrics. Distracts from the research content and overshoots the academic register.

The goal: excellent design with natural flow. Not safe mediocrity, not visual theater. Distinctive and credible.

## Design Principles

1. **Substance drives structure** — credentials and content determine every layout decision. Decoration earns its place or doesn't appear.
2. **Authority without distance** — the voice is precise and expert, but never cold. Collaboration feels possible.
3. **Earned craft** — quality is felt in spacing, typography, and color, not announced through effects. The site should prompt "how was this made?" not "which template is this?"
4. **International readability** — scannable at all viewport sizes for a global audience. Line lengths, contrast, and hierarchy are non-negotiable.
5. **Coherent flow** — each section leads logically to the next. No dead ends, no orphaned content, no sections that exist only because every academic site has them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

English-first. WCAG AA minimum: 4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large text. Reduced motion honored. Arabic content (name, affiliations) should render cleanly within Latin-primary layout.
