How I teach

  • Student-centred learning. Interactive lectures and lab sessions that tie complex concepts to real-world systems and current threats.
  • Outcome-based design. Syllabi, lesson plans and assessments mapped to programme learning outcomes and accreditation criteria.
  • Hands-on practice. Labs, cloud sandboxes and security exercises so students build skills, not just knowledge.
  • Feedback and assessment. Varied assessment methods with timely, constructive feedback that supports each student's progress.
  • Technology in the classroom. Learning platforms, e-classrooms and online modules that raise engagement and reach.
  • Mentoring. Academic and career advising, project supervision, and links with industry for practical exposure.
