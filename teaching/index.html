Majmaah University

Teaching

Dr. Ahmad Raza Khan is an Associate Professor in the College of Computer and Information Sciences at Majmaah University. This page lists the teaching and academic-support work already described on this site, plus one workshop named on the university’s public pages. It is not a semester timetable.

Academic support at Majmaah University

On this site, his consultation work for university deanships includes:

  • Deanship of E-Learning: e-classrooms with smart interactive screens, an e-course for Programming II, and system administration for Blackboard.
  • Deanship of IT: server virtualization, technical infrastructure, and IT systems integration.
  • Deanship of Educational Affairs: data analysis and reporting, educational program assessment, and strategic planning.

Workshop on the public record

VMware virtualization and cloud computing at CCIS

The College of Computer and Information Sciences lists a workshop on 21 October 2020 by Dr. Ahmad Raza Khan, Department of Information Technology: “VMWare Virtualization and Cloud Computing resources at CCIS awareness and HandsOn workshop.”
